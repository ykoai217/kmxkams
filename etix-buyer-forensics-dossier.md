# ETIX Buyer Forensics: Factual Coverage Dossier

**As of:** 27 September 2026
**Subject:** ETIX (EThical Internet eXchange), formerly Etix Everywhere. Registered head office: Saint-Herblain (Nantes), France
**Scope:** Factual reconstruction of identity, perimeter, ownership, assets, pipeline, financing, customers and management. This document contains no acquisition recommendation, no target identification, no ranking and no M&A thesis. Interpretations appear only in Section 22 and are labelled **I**.

## 0. Method, labels and collection limitation (read first)

**Evidence labels.** **O** Observed (stated in a source) · **D** Derived (arithmetic or logic from O facts, method shown) · **I** Inferred (interpretation, flagged) · **U** Unknown (searched, not found).

**Source confidence.** **H**: at least two independent sources report the same figure or wording. **M**: one primary source (company, sponsor, regulator, law firm, registry) read through a search-index extract, or several strong secondary sources agree. **L**: single secondary source, aggregator, social post, or an extract with a visible extraction problem.

**Collection limitation.** The environment's egress proxy blocked direct retrieval of every relevant domain tried: etixeverywhere.com, apac.etixeverywhere.com, infraviacapital.com, realassets.ipe.com, cliffordchance.com, presse.bpifrance.fr, bebeez.eu, thetechcapital.com, w.media, datacentermap.com, datacenters.com, announcements.asx.com.au, globaldatacentres.com.au, aspecthuntley.com.au, ec.europa.eu and annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr (plus the recherche-entreprises API). **Every fact in this dossier was captured through search-index extracts of those pages.** No document was read directly. Consistent with the brief, confidence is capped at **M** unless two independent sources agree on the same figure. Search extraction can paraphrase, mis-date or mis-attribute; several such errors were caught and are logged in Section 21.

**Currency warning.** Several press items (for example DCD on the Eurazeo deal) label Australian-dollar figures from Global Data Centre Group (GDC, ASX-listed) filings as US dollars. This dossier states the currency used by the originating filing wherever it can be traced. Where a euro equivalent is derived, the FX rate implied by GDC's own disclosure (A$87.4m = €56.0m, i.e. ~1.56 A$/€) is used and labelled **D**.

**Perimeter warning.** "Etix" names two different corporate lineages:
- **Legacy Etix Everywhere (2012 to Feb 2020):** a Luxembourg-founded developer with a global footprint (Europe, Africa, Latin America, Nordics, blockchain/HPC). Acquired by Vantage Data Centers in February 2020.
- **Current ETIX (Dec 2020 to date):** the French, Belgian and Colombian edge perimeter plus the "Etix Edge" brand, bought back from Vantage in a management buyout backed by 360 Capital (later Global Data Centre Group) in December 2020, then extended into Thailand and across France.

Facts about the legacy company are kept in historical tables and are not attributed to today's ETIX unless a source links them.

## 1. Executive factual state

1. **O/H.** Vantage Data Centers acquired Etix Everywhere on 10 Feb 2020 from InfraVia, Tiger Infrastructure, SBI, STOA and Keppel Data Centres. At that date Etix had 12 data centres, more than 50 MW built capacity, more than 200 clients and a 55 MW hyperscale campus under construction in Frankfurt. Terms were not disclosed. [S1, S2, S3, S4, S5]
2. **O/M.** In December 2020 the managers of Etix's French subsidiary, led by Louis Blanchot, completed an MBO with 360 Capital Digital Infrastructure Fund (ASX: TDI, later Global Data Centre Group, ASX: GDC). They bought from Vantage the "Etix Edge" brand and the data centres in France, Belgium and Colombia. GDC's announced investment was A$37.5m. The perimeter was two facilities at 100% and six at 50% with JV partners. [S6, S7, S8, S9]
3. **O/M.** The principal French holding company is **Etix Everywhere Holding France SAS** (SIREN 891 040 958), created 3 Nov 2020, registered at 2 Impasse Joséphine Baker, 44800 Saint-Herblain. Share capital was €118,220,822 after an October 2024 increase from €115,071,923. [S38]
4. **O/H.** ETIX is **jointly controlled by Infranity Equity Fund SCA SICAV-RAIF and Eurazeo Transition Infrastructure Fund (ETIF)**. The European Commission cleared the acquisition of joint control of "Etix Everywhere Holding" on 16 Aug 2024 (case M.11618, simplified procedure, Art. 6(1)(b)). Exact percentages are **U**. [S21, S20, S28]
5. **O/M.** Eurazeo entered in February 2023. It injected an initial A$46.8m (**D:** ~€30m) for a ~30% stake at a pre-money value of A$116.6m (**D:** ~€75m), diluting GDC to ~70%. [S13, S14]
6. **O/H.** Infranity signed to buy GDC's stake on 21 May 2024 and closed on 3 to 4 Sep 2024. GDC reported final net proceeds of A$175.6m (€108m), a 52% uplift on its A$115m carrying value at 31 Dec 2023. [S19, S20, S22]
7. **O/M.** The brand changed from "Etix Everywhere" to **"ETIX" (EThical Internet eXchange)**, announced 11 Sep 2025. Company wording: "In the past, ETIX was 'Everywhere', we are today proudly anchored in France and turned towards Europe", backed by "exclusively European investors, Infranity and Eurazeo". [S30]
8. **O/M.** In October 2025 the refinancing announcement stated that ETIX "currently operates 14 data centers in France and Belgium with a deployed capacity of 8.1 MW". [S28, S29]
9. **O/M.** On 16 Jun 2026 ETIX closed the purchase of Eurofiber France's data-centre business: three wholly owned facilities (Toulouse, Auch, Nîmes), a colocation unit in Labège and four staff. The company says this took it from 15 to 19 data centres globally and to six locations in Occitanie. The price is **U**. [S33]
10. **O/M.** Thailand: ETIX Bangkok #1 (ex-Genesis) has been 100% owned since a November 2024 share purchase of Interlink Telecom's 33%. Company-stated capacity is 5 MW, of which 3 MW was in use in August 2025. [S10, S25, S66]
11. **O/M.** ETIX Bangkok #2 is a **23 MW design-IT** building of 16,000 m². Core and shell were completed and announced in August 2025, with first capacity "available in September 2026". The campus total is stated at 28 MW (5 + 23). [S27, S34]
12. **U.** No source found confirms that BKK#2 was energised or had any customer contracted at 27 Sep 2026.
13. **O/H.** October 2025 refinancing: €120m committed (amortising facility plus RCF) with an extra €50m of additional capacity (total up to €170m). Lenders: BNP Paribas, Bpifrance, Kommunalkredit Austria, La Banque Postale and **MUFG**. Société Générale CIB advised; Clifford Chance advised ETIX. Stated use: €50m to refinance existing debt, €70m for development, €50m available for acquisitions or expansion. [S28, S29]
14. **O/M.** June 2026: ETIX Thailand signed a credit facility of **up to US$70m with Muzinich & Co.** A first tranche of US$21.5m finances BKK#2. Index Partners advised ETIX Thailand; A&O Shearman and Baker McKenzie were counsel to borrower and lenders respectively. [S34]
15. **O/M.** The only documented **direct** MUFG relationship is its membership of the October 2025 five-bank syndicate. MUFG's title (MLA, agent, hedging bank) is **U**. [S28, S29]
16. **O/M.** Explicit growth statements: a regional "cluster" strategy; five new sites; ambition to double the network by 2030 "by combining openings and strategic acquisitions"; entry into Spain, Italy, Switzerland and Germany (Forbes also lists the Netherlands); a stated ambition to reach 30 data centres; and (May 2024) 30 MW of installed IT capacity by 2030. [S19, S20, S29]
17. **O/M.** Revenue: CFNEWS described ETIX in 2024 as a company "with €32 million in revenue" (period **U**). No audited consolidated revenue, EBITDA or net debt was found. [S23]
18. **O/M.** ETIX invested "at least €50m" in 2025. The company's own cost benchmark is €10m to €12m per MW of IT capacity. [S29]
19. **O/M.** Growth since 2018 has come mainly through acquisitions of operating companies or businesses: NeoCenter Ouest (2018), Genesis 67% (2022), a Couëron data centre (2022), CIV France (€56m, 2022), zColo France (~€63m, 2023), HosTELyon (<€5m, 2024), Interlink's 33% (2024) and Eurofiber France DC business (2026). Documented organic builds: Lille #1 (2017, co-financed), Lille #4 (2025 to 2026), BKK#1 modules and BKK#2. [Section 13, 14]
20. **O/M.** Named customers: E.Leclerc, Auchan, Carrefour (Forbes France, October 2025); Oodrive (Île-de-France sites, 2026); an unnamed "global AI partner" deploying next-generation GPUs at BKK#1 (October 2025). Eurofiber keeps access to capacity through a commercial partnership. The company claims more than 500 clients. No contracted MW, tenor or concentration data found. [S29, S36, S51, S33]
21. **O/M.** JV history: Lille #1 (co-financed with Eura DC, 2017), Nantes #2 (50/50 with Groupe Sigma, 2019), Belgium DC (50/50 with NRB, 2017), Cali #1 (with Compunet), Bangkok #1 (67/33 with Interlink until 2024). The **current** ETIX interest in Lille #1, Nantes #2 and Belgium DC is **U**. [S41, S42, S43, S47, S10]
22. **O/M.** Leadership: Louis Blanchot (Group CEO; first employee in 2012; led the 2020 MBO); Michel Brignano (Chairman since 2021; ex-Managing Director Equinix France, Telecity, Global Switch); Thomas Hombert (Managing Director France); Jean-Baptiste Lamy (COO since 2023); Quentin Lorthiois (Finance Director since 2020); Pierre Patris (CEO Asia since 2020). Headcount 85 (January 2026). [S37, S32, S58]
23. **O/M.** Technical positioning: Lille #4 and BKK#2 are marketed for racks up to 150 kW with direct liquid cooling (BKK#2 also immersion). Most legacy French sites are listed at up to 12 kW per cabinet. [S32, S27, S52]
24. **O/M.** Sovereignty: ETIX committed its 13 French data centres to the CISPE Sovereignty Certification in 2026 (preliminary audit passed; six-month in-depth audit). [S35]
25. **O/M.** Legacy footprints exited or transferred: Frankfurt hyperscale campus (to Vantage, 2020), Iceland Borealis/Etix Blockchain (Vauban majority, 2021), Ghana Etix Accra #1 (now Onix Accra 1 under AIIM), Colombia Etix Cali #1 (now Wave DC under Grupo Cotel by 2024). The status of Sweden (Jokkmokk JV) is **U**. [S5, S45, S46, S47]
26. **U.** EBITDA, net debt, leverage, covenants, contracted MW, occupancy (other than BKK#1's 3 of 5 MW) and grid contracts for any pipeline MW were not found.
