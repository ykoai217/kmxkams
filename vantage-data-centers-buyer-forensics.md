## 0. Method, labels and collection limits (read first)

**Evidence labels.** **O** Observed (stated by a source). **D** Derived (arithmetic or logic on O facts; the working is shown). **I** Inferred (interpretation; recorded only in Section 28, and body text points to the parked item by number). **U** Unknown.

**Confidence.** **H** directly retrieved primary source, preferably corroborated. **M** primary wording available through search-index extraction, or several strong sources agree. **L** one secondary source, aggregator, social post, ambiguous extract or conference bio.

**Collection limitation (material).** The session's egress policy blocked direct retrieval of every relevant host tested: vantage-dc.com, sec.gov, digitalbridge.com, businesswire.com, prnewswire.com, datacenterdynamics.com, spglobal.com, scoperatings.com, dbrs.morningstar.com, asreport.americanbanker.com, cnbc.com, creditflowresearch.com and wikipedia.org. Every fact here was captured through a search index that extracts or summarises those pages. No page was read directly. **For that reason no claim in this dossier is graded H.** Primary-source claims reached through the index, or corroborated by two independent outlets, are capped at **M**. Single-outlet, aggregator or paraphrase-risk claims are **L**. Search extraction can paraphrase, merge two documents or mis-attribute a number. Where that risk was visible (for example a customer statistic whose source document is ambiguous), the claim is graded L and logged in Section 27.

**Perimeter discipline.** "Vantage" is split in this file into at least eight distinct perimeters: (1) the global operating and management company; (2) Vantage North America development platform; (3) Vantage SDC (stabilized North American carve-out) and its ABS master trust; (4) the "Retained" North American ABS trust; (5) Vantage EMEA platform; (6) the EMEA stabilized investment partnership and its two ABS issuers; (7) Vantage APAC platform; (8) campus-level financing silos (Phoenix CMBS, New Albany construction loan, Frontier and Lighthouse bank debt, development warehouse). Figures are never added across these perimeters unless the text says so and shows the basis.

**Unit discipline.** MW means critical IT MW unless the source says utility, grid, MVA or "power". "Design", "planned", "secured" and "operational" are reported in the words the source used.

## 1. EXECUTIVE FACTUAL STATE

1. **O/M.** Vantage launched on 17 June 2010, seeded by Silver Lake, by acquiring a former Intel data center in Santa Clara. First CEO was Jim Trout. [S2]
2. **O/M.** On 27 March 2017 a consortium of Digital Bridge Holdings, LLC, PSP Investments and TIAA Investments (Nuveen affiliate) bought Vantage from Silver Lake. Terms were not disclosed; press at the time cited about US$1bn (L). [S1]
3. **O/M.** Sureel Choksi has been President and CEO since 2013. Regional presidents are Dana Adams (North America, Feb 2024), David Howson (EMEA, Feb 2024) and Jeremy Deutsch (APAC, Oct 2024). Scott Beasley became global CFO on 24 March 2026. [S61]
4. **O/M.** Vantage is controlled through vehicles managed by DigitalBridge, with Silver Lake as co-lead equity since 2024. DigitalBridge itself is being acquired by SoftBank Group ($16.00/share, EV ~US$4.0bn). All regulatory approvals (including CFIUS and FERC) were received on 22 Sep 2026, with closing expected within five business days. Completion was **not** observed as of 27 Sep 2026 (U). [S14]
5. **O/M.** The June 2024 equity round closed at **US$9.2bn** (announced at US$6.4bn, upsized by US$2.8bn), led by DigitalBridge and Silver Lake vehicles. With AustralianSuper's €1.5bn it formed ~US$11bn of new investment in nine months, of which "more than US$7bn" was primary equity (so ~US$4bn was secondary, **D**). [S6, S7, S66]
6. **O/M.** PSP Investments sold the majority of its Vantage investment at the June 2024 closing. It had been an investor since 2017. [S8]
7. **O/M.** Regional equity partners are separately layered: AustralianSuper (€1.5bn, "significant minority" of Vantage EMEA, Sep 2023); GIC and MEAG (€1.4bn into EMEA, Dec 2024, GIC's first EMEA investment); GIC and ADIA (US$1.6bn into APAC, completed Nov 2025). Percentages are not public (U). [S9, S11, S12]
8. **O/M.** Two "stabilized-asset" partnerships sit beside the development platforms. **Vantage SDC** (North America, formed July 2020, 12 then 13 stabilized data centers, 177MW) and an **EMEA Investment Partnership** (six stabilized European data centers, 177MW, valued ~€2.5bn including Vantage's stake, closed Oct 2023, led by MEAG and Infranity with DigitalBridge funds). [S3, S10, S20]
9. **O/M.** DigitalBridge deconsolidated Vantage SDC on 31 Dec 2023 after governance changes. It no longer controls the SDC Board of Managers. Its two consolidated funds held 38.3% of SDC (DigitalBridge balance sheet 12.8%, fund LPs 25.6%), fair-valued at US$393.8m. [S4]
10. **O/M.** Reuters and Bloomberg (13 Aug 2026) report early talks on an IPO at ~US$100bn valuation raising ~US$10bn, or a sale or stake sale, as soon as 2027. No formal process was reported. [S13]
11. **O/M.** Current footprint language varies by source: "35 campuses on five continents" (Reuters, Aug 2026); ">2.6GW planned and existing, 14 countries, 21 markets" (2025 boilerplate); "17 North American campuses, 4GW+ of IT load capacity" (Ares, Feb 2026); "~2.5GW operational or in development" in EMEA (Dec 2024); "1GW operational and planned" in APAC (Nov 2025). These use different perimeters and stages and are **not** additive. [S13, S37, S11, S12, S77]
12. **O/M.** The two largest projects are OpenAI/Oracle "Stargate" sites: **Frontier** (Shackelford County, Texas; 1.4GW; 1,200 acres; 10 buildings; >US$25bn; first building targeted 2H 2026) and **Lighthouse** (Port Washington, Wisconsin; "close to a gigawatt", ~902MW IT per one source; 672 acres; four buildings; >US$15bn; completion 2028). [S39, S41]
13. **O/M.** A ~US$38bn bank debt package for Frontier (~US$23.25bn) and Lighthouse (~US$14.75bn) was led by **JPMorgan and MUFG** (Bloomberg, Sep to Nov 2025). A Vantage spokesperson confirmed it funds the two Vantage campuses. Reported terms: four years plus two one-year extensions, about SOFR+250bp, ~1% top-tier upfront fee for commitments of US$300m or more. Final close, borrower entity and security are **U**. [S40]
14. **O/M.** MUFG and Société Générale co-led the **US$2.25bn New Albany (Ohio) construction loan** (June 2025), described as one of the first data center construction loans with private investment-grade ratings from two agencies. [S36]
15. **O/M.** North American revolving "borrowing base" green loan: US$3.0bn (Apr 2024, Wells Fargo structuring; TD, Truist, Scotiabank), upsized by US$2.75bn in June 2025. The initial pool was eight leased and greenfield sites of ~1.4GW. [S35, S36]
16. **O/M.** Ares arranged a **US$2.4bn** facility (Feb 2026; Ares ~US$1.6bn, ~US$330m funded at close) for North American refinancing and development. Ares stated the stabilized NA portfolio is "100% leased" to hyperscalers and "~99%" of NA customers are investment grade. [S37]
17. **O/M.** On 14 Sep 2026 Vantage closed a **US$2bn development-stage warehouse** (five-year revolver incl. extensions; three initial development assets; Evercore and Wells Fargo lead arrangers; about a dozen insurance and institutional investors). Vantage said it had closed ">US$40bn of capital during 2026". [S38]
18. **O/M.** Vantage runs **two separate North American ABS master trusts**: (a) Vantage Data Centers Issuer, LLC / Vantage Data Centers Canada L.P. (depositor Vantage Data Centers Holdings, LLC), secured by 13 stabilized DCs (8 Santa Clara, 2 Quincy, 1 Montreal, 2 Quebec City); (b) Retained Vantage Data Centers Issuer, LLC (sponsor Retained Vantage Data Centers, LP), whose US$1.35bn 2023 issue refinanced three Northern Virginia DCs and five Quebec DCs. [S26, S27, S28, S30, S31]
19. **D/M.** The 13-DC composition of trust (a) matches Vantage SDC's 13 stabilized DCs (12 at formation plus CA22 in Oct 2021). Trust (a) is therefore the SDC financing perimeter. [S20, S30]
20. **O/M.** Vantage issued the first EMEA data center ABS (**£600m**, June 2024, Vantage Data Centers Jersey Borrower SPV Limited, two Newport DCs, 54.8% LTV at closing), tapped by **£254m** in Jan 2026, and the first euro data center ABS (**€640m** funded plus €80m VFN, June 2025, Vantage Data Centers Germany Borrower Lux S.à r.l., four German DCs valued €928m). [S32, S33, S34]
21. **O/L.** One S&P-derived extract for the North American master trust (a) reports the largest tenant at 59.7% of leased capacity and 56.4% of annualised base rent, top five at 84.8%, and the largest tenant rated AAA. The series date for this extract is unclear. [S27, S30]
22. **O/M.** Publicly named customers are limited: **Oracle** (tenant, with **OpenAI** as end user) at Frontier and Lighthouse; Oracle leasing the first 98MW at the ex-Yondr Johor campus for 15 years with 2% annual escalation (L); historic Microsoft (10MW, Santa Clara) and Box leases. Most leases are reported as unnamed "hyperscale customers". [S39, S41, S69, S74]
23. **O/M.** Oracle's own filings show US$260bn of data center lease commitments not yet commenced at 31 May 2026, with 15 to 19 year terms. Vantage is not named. S&P cut Oracle to BBB- on 9 Jul 2026. [S67]
24. **O/M.** Corporate acquisitions: 4Degrees (C$259m, closed Jan 2019); Etix Everywhere (Feb 2020, sellers Infravia and Tiger Infrastructure); NGD Newport (Jul 2020); Hypertec hyperscale business (Nov 2020); Agile Data Centers (Sep 2021); PCCW DC (~US$750m, closed Dec 2021); Yondr's Johor campus (closed 24 Nov 2025). Etix's edge business was sold back to management in Dec 2020. [S15 to S19, S12]
25. **O/M.** Vantage has explored disposals in APAC: four Hong Kong DCs (US$300m to US$500m, Bloomberg Apr 2024; outcome U) and its Malaysian assets (>US$2bn, Bloomberg 29 Jul 2026; no decision). [S56, S57]
26. **O/M.** Power strategy includes on-site gas: VA2 (Loudoun) runs 24/7 on natural gas turbines as primary power; VoltaGrid partnership for >1GW of gas microgrid generation (Feb 2025); Liberty Energy partnership for up to 1GW, including 400MW reserved 2027 generation (Jan 2026); DUB1 has a 100MVA on-site multi-fuel plant. [S45, S59, S60, S51]
27. **O/M.** Lighthouse is served by We Energies under a Very Large Customer tariff approved in April 2026 (15-year minimum term; credit support below A-). Oracle is suing the Wisconsin PSC over the collateral rule. MISO set 1 Dec 2027 for transmission upgrades. Vantage sought a 1.3GW connection. [S41]
28. **O/M.** Planning and grid friction is documented at Dublin (DUB13 refused, upheld Apr 2025; €151.7m estimated SLA credits for delayed RFS at DUB11 per Jul 2026 filings) and Frankfurt (Groß-Gerau council refused FRA5, 174MW, Feb 2026). [S51, S52]
29. **O/M.** New EMEA entries since 2025: Zaragoza, Spain (€3.2bn, 90MW supply granted by Endesa); Bordeaux, France with Altarea (400MW connection secured by Altarea, Feb 2026); second Milan campus (32MW); Bridgend, Wales (former Ford plant). [S53, S54, S50]
30. **U.** Revenue, EBITDA, net debt, leverage and consolidated capex are not publicly disclosed for any Vantage perimeter. No rating-agency corporate rating was found; only structured-finance ratings exist.
31. **O/M.** No MUFG role was found in any Vantage ABS, the NA borrowing-base facility, the Ares facility, the US$2bn warehouse, the EMEA €750m private credit facility or the APAC bank loans. MUFG's evidenced roles are New Albany (co-lead) and Frontier/Lighthouse (co-lead). [S36, S40]

### 9.2 Reconciliation

1. **2.6GW (2025 boilerplate) vs 4GW+ NA (Feb 2026).** One outlet printed the 2.6GW boilerplate in the same article as Frontier. If 2.6GW included Frontier (1.4GW), every other global site would total ~1.2GW (D), which cannot be reconciled with Ares' 4GW+ for North America alone six months later. The boilerplate's cut-off date is U. Unresolved; logged in Section 27.
2. **4GW+ NA (Ares) cross-check (D, M-L).** Sum of stated NA design figures: Frontier 1,400 + Lighthouse ~902 + VA1 206 + VA2 96 + VA3 288 + VA4 192 + Santa Clara 216 + AZ1 176 + OH1 192 + NV1 224 + Quebec (QC1 11 + QC4 ~48 + QC2 86 + QC6 U) ~145 = ~4,037MW, before Quincy, Pittsboro (400) and Georgia. This is consistent with "4GW+" on a design basis. It is not operating capacity.
3. **Global addition is not valid.** NA 4GW+ (Feb 2026, IT, mixed stage) + EMEA ~2.5GW (Dec 2024, IT, operational or in development) + APAC ~1GW (Nov 2025, IT, operational or planned) = ~7.5GW (D) is **not** a platform total: dates differ, stage definitions differ, EMEA includes stabilized-partnership MW not wholly owned, and APAC may be partly sold.
4. **Owned vs JV.** SDC (177MW) and the EMEA partnership (177MW) are operated by Vantage but owned with third parties. Whether platform headline figures include them is U.
5. **IT vs utility.** Only Lighthouse (1.3GW grid request vs ~902MW IT) and Bordeaux (400MW grid connection) give electrical figures. The implied Lighthouse grid-to-IT ratio is ~1.44 (D). No other utility MW found.
6. **Secured vs energised.** No Vantage source uses "secured power" with a definition. "Granted" (Zaragoza) and "secured a power connection" (Bordeaux, secured by Altarea) are recorded verbatim. Energised MW is U everywhere.
