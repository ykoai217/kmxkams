On January 24, 2019, Teraco Data Environments announced that Berkshire Partners LLC, a Boston-based investment firm, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire a majority stake in the company. The Permira funds, an existing shareholder, remained a significant investor. Teraco, based in Johannesburg and established in 2008, is the largest provider of datacentre services in Africa. Five high-quality facilities with 30MW of critical power load serving more than 450 clients across five core ecosystems: telecoms, managed service providers, content, enterprise, and financial services. The Permira funds initially backed a Teraco management buyout in December 2014; the business has multiplied its capacity more than six times. Subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions; expected close in the first quarter of 2019.
