Larry Hamelsky, a Managing Director at Berkshire Partners: "We believe that Teraco is exceptionally well-positioned to capitalize on the fast growth of the Sub-Saharan datacentre infrastructure market given its highly interconnected ecosystems, well-designed facilities, and ability to offer a wide array of deployments." Berkshire Partners LLC, a Boston-based investment firm, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire a majority stake in Teraco; expected close Q1 2019.
