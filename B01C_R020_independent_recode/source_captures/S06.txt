Berkshire Partners LLC acquired a stake in Teraco Data Environments Pty Ltd., becoming the biggest shareholder in Africa's largest data-center to capitalize on growing demand for internet and cloud-based services on the continent. Teraco was expected to fetch between $600 million and $1 billion, including debt; the value of the transaction wasn't disclosed. Teraco planning to double installed capacity to 60 megawatts in the coming four to five years.
