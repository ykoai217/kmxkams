**As of:** 27 September 2026
**Subject:** EdgeConneX, Inc. and its controlled, jointly controlled and minority perimeters
**Purpose:** Buyer factual-state file for the data-centre buyer-universe build. Input to later hypothesis generation against the historical transaction engine.

## Reading notes (method, labels, collection limitation)

**Evidence labels.** **O** Observed (stated by a source). **D** Derived (arithmetic or logic from O facts, method shown). **I** Inferred (interpretation; appears only in Section 29 or where explicitly flagged). **U** Unknown.

**Confidence.** **H** primary document body read directly and corroborated. **M** primary-source wording available through search-index extraction, or several strong independent sources agree. **L** single secondary source, aggregator, social post, or visible extraction risk.

**Collection limitation (binding on every grade in this file).** The research environment's egress proxy blocked direct retrieval of every primary domain attempted: edgeconnex.com, eqtgroup.com, mb.cision.com, prnewswire.com, provequity.com, sec.gov, govinfo.gov, adanienterprises.com, resources.adaniconnex.com, cliffordchance.com, datacenterdynamics.com, ionanalytics.com, esgnews.com, baxtel.com and datacentermap.com. All evidence was captured through a search index that extracts and summarises page content. Consequences:

1. **No claim in this file is graded H.** The ceiling is **M**, even where an issuer's own press release is the underlying document.
2. Where the index summary and the underlying document could differ (figures, dates, roles), the claim is graded **M** only if two independent sources agree, otherwise **L**.
3. Several index summaries mixed EdgeConneX with look-alike companies. These were caught and excluded: **EdgeCore Digital Infrastructure** (US hyperscale developer, separate company), **EDGNEX** (DAMAC-owned, Madrid), **EdgeUno** (LatAm edge network), **Edge Group** (UAE defence), **Csquare/Centersquare** (a US$603.4m 42-site acquisition that one summary wrongly attributed to EdgeConneX), and **EQT Corporation** (US gas producer, unrelated to EQT AB).
4. Aggregator figures (Tracxn, Growjo, Sparkco, Owler, Revelio) are logged in the conflict register and never used as canonical values.

**Perimeter vocabulary used throughout.**
- **ECX-WO:** EdgeConneX wholly owned or controlled perimeter (as far as public evidence shows).
- **ECX-JV:** joint ventures where EdgeConneX holds a stated or presumed partial interest (Aboitiz, Stockland, TA Realty development arrangement).
- **ECX-MIN:** minority investment (Chayora).
- **ACX:** AdaniConneX, a 50:50 JV. Always reported separately. Never added to ECX-WO figures.
- **Sponsor level:** EQT funds, the EQT AI Infrastructure vehicle, continuation vehicle, Sixth Street and other equity holders above EdgeConneX, Inc.

**MW vocabulary.** "IT MW" = critical IT load. "Utility MW" or "power" = grid or site power. "Design MW" = full build-out. "Gas MW" = on-site generation nameplate. Where a source does not say which, the table says "undefined".

## 1. EXECUTIVE FACTUAL STATE

1. **O/M.** EdgeConneX was founded in 2009 in Herndon, Northern Virginia, by **Randy Brouckman (CEO)** and **Edmund Wilson (COO)**. Both remain in role and on the board in 2026. [S16, S17]
2. **O/M.** Its first business was edge colocation anchored by cable and broadband networks. **Comcast and Cox** were described as both early customers and investors. Akamai and Ciena were also early strategic investors. [S60]
3. **O/M.** **Providence Equity Partners** invested around 2015 (Fund VII, 2012 vintage, per PitchBook) and led the seller group in 2020. [S3, S5]
4. **O/M.** **EQT Infrastructure IV** agreed to acquire EdgeConneX on 19 Aug 2020 and closed on 5 Nov 2020. The HSR acquiring person was **EQT Infrastructure IV (No. 1) EUR SCSp** (FTC early termination 20201430). Price undisclosed; press reported **US$2.5bn to US$3bn** (one outlet: US$2.6bn). [S1, S2, S4, S5]
5. **O/M.** At acquisition EdgeConneX had **"over 40 facilities in 33 markets"** across North America, Europe and South America. [S1]
6. **O/M.** **EQT Infrastructure V** co-invested on 19 Aug 2021. After closing, Funds IV and V held **equal ownership stakes**. [S6]
7. **O/M.** **EQT Infrastructure VI** agreed on 5 Jan 2024 to "partner with EdgeConneX" for hyperscale development ("hundreds of megawatts"). The legal form of the Fund VI investment (shares in EdgeConneX, Inc. or a separate development vehicle) is **U**. [S7]
8. **O/M.** On 24 Sep 2024, Funds IV and V agreed to sell a **minority stake to Sixth Street**. EQT stayed the largest shareholder. Stake size and price are **U**. [S8]
9. **O/M.** On the FY2025 call (22 Jan 2026), EQT announced its first **open-ended continuation vehicle for EdgeConneX**. Trade press reports **Blackstone Strategic Partners** as lead investor and a vehicle of about **US$2bn** at establishment. [S9, S10]
10. **O/M.** On 21 Apr 2026, EQT launched an **AI Infrastructure strategy**, "fully seeded" by EdgeConneX through the purchase of **minority stakes from Infra IV and Infra V**. It is open-ended, with fees on NAV. [S11, S12]
11. **O/M.** **CPP Investments** committed **US$1.75bn (C$2.4bn)** to the EQT AI Infrastructure strategy on about 3 Jul 2026. The transaction closed. [S13]
12. **O/L.** Globes reports that Israeli institutions (Migdal US$350m, More US$330m, Meitav US$250m, Clal US$100m) invested about **US$1.03bn**, described as ~15% of **US$6bn** invested in the company since the start of 2026. The vehicle is not named. [S14]
13. **U.** Current ownership percentages across EQT IV, V, VI, the AI Infrastructure vehicle, the continuation vehicle, Sixth Street, the Israeli institutions, CPP and management are **not public**.
14. **O/M.** Chairman is **Michael Tobin CBE** (Tobin Ventures). The board includes EQT partners, the two co-founders and a Sixth Street co-founder and partner. [S16]
15. **O/M.** **AdaniConneX (ACX)** was formed on 23 Feb 2021 as a **50:50 JV between Adani Enterprises Ltd and EdgeConneX**. It remained 50:50 through mid-2026 in AEL materials. [S18, S22]
16. **O/M.** ACX reported **55+ MW operational** at end FY26 (31 Mar 2026) and **560+ MW cumulative "tied-up" capacity**, including a new **358 MW hyperscale order in Hyderabad**. These are ACX figures, 100% basis. [S22]
17. **O/M.** ACX's debt is separate: **US$213m** (Jun 2023), a **US$1.44bn** framework (Apr 2024; US$875m committed plus accordion), and an **US$800m 18-month bridge** (Aug 2026, ~SOFR+200). **MUFG Bank** is a lender on all three. [S19, S20, S21]
18. **O/M.** The core European debt is a sustainability-linked **€2.4bn** portfolio facility (Oct 2022; 12 operating sites, 7 jurisdictions; term loan, capex facility and RCF), upsized by **US$1.9bn (€1.75bn)** in Apr 2024. Underwriters in 2022: **ABN AMRO, ING, Natixis, SMBC, Société Générale**. MUFG is not named. [S28, S31]
19. **O/M.** EdgeConneX runs **three securitisation programmes**: US ABS Series 2022-1 (**US$375m Class A-2, DBRS BBB**, confirmed 25 Aug 2026), green ABS 2024-1 (**US$150m**), and a Dutch euro master-trust ABS **ECX 2026-1 (€707.5m, Mar 2026)**, which Fitch downgraded about a week after pricing. [S32, S33, S34]
20. **O/M.** US hyperscale campuses are increasingly **project-financed**. Atlanta: **US$300m bridge (Q3 2024)** and a **US$2.2bn to US$2.3bn** project financing launched by Natixis, SMBC and MUFG. New Albany, Ohio: **~US$3bn** marketed in Apr 2026 by Natixis, MUFG and SocGen, bundling data centre and on-site gas assets. Cedar Creek, Texas: **up to US$4bn** under consideration (Aug 2026). [S35, S36, S37]
21. **O/M.** In Aug 2026 EdgeConneX sought a **US$2.5bn letter-of-credit facility** to guarantee power costs. Natixis and BBVA were named as banks in talks. [S38]
22. **O/M.** **MUFG direct relationship:** MLA, account bank, facility agent, security agent and hedging bank on the **Jakarta SLL** (Sep 2023, borrower PT Graha Teknologi Nusantara); lender on the Atlanta bridge; underwriter on Atlanta PF; lead bank on New Albany. Stated roles only. [S30, S35, S36]
23. **O/M.** Power strategy includes **on-site gas** through affiliate **PowerConneX**. New Albany: PCX I (up to 140 MW; OPSB certificate 22 Jul 2025), PCX II (up to 216 MW; certificate 20 Nov 2025), PCX III (up to 430 MW; pre-application). Ashville, Ohio: 800 MW gas plant pending OPSB. Dublin: ~100 MW on-site gas generation. [S39, S41, S55]
24. **O/M.** Named customer evidence (ECX perimeter): **Lambda** (issuer-confirmed; ATL02, Chicago, new 23 MW Chicago build), **Microsoft** (acknowledged end user at Union City, Georgia), **CoreWeave** (Cedar Creek Building 1, local-press and tax-abatement evidence), **Meta** (New Albany campus "developed for Meta", per lender-marketing press only), **Liberty Global** (UK anchor tenant, 2016). [S42, S43, S44, S36, S60]
25. **U.** Customer count, concentration, top-five share, contracted MW, WALT, occupancy and lease terms are **not public** for the ECX perimeter.
26. **O/M.** The only disclosed platform capacity metric is **410 MW "installed capacity" at end-2024** (2024 Sustainability Report). The MW definition (IT vs utility) is **U**. [S61]
27. **O/M.** Sponsor-stated growth: capacity "more than tripled" by Jan 2024; "nearly 20x" since 2020 by Apr to Jul 2026; **">10 GW" of additional development planned**. None of these is split into operating, construction, contracted or secured-power MW. [S7, S11, S13]
28. **O/M.** Site count claims: "80 data centers in operation or development, >50 markets" (2024); ">90 data centers built and in development, >60 markets" (2026). [S7, S8, S12, S17]
29. **O/M.** Wholly owned APAC: **Jakarta** (bought GTN from Multipolar and Mitsui; completed 28 Apr 2022; US$37.61m; campus 120 MW rising to 200+ MW), **Malaysia** (~300 MW across KL CBD, Bukit Jalil, Cyberjaya; entry Sep 2023), **Japan** (350 MW utility power across two Greater Osaka sites; entry Jan 2025). [S45, S46, S47]
30. **O/M.** APAC partnerships outside India: **Chayora (China)** strategic minority investment (Dec 2021; Actis kept majority); **Aboitiz InfraCapital (Philippines)** JV (Oct 2022; split U); **Stockland (Australia)** 50:50 JV (Mar 2026). [S48, S49, S50]
31. **O/M.** EMEA operating markets: Dublin, Amsterdam, Brussels (Ternat), Frankfurt, Munich, Barcelona, Warsaw, Milan, Israel (Herzliya, Petah Tikva, Rishon). Development: Heusenstamm (€600m), Italy "Campus Italia" (€3bn, >300 MW by 2031, declared of national strategic interest 14 May 2026), Skellefteå (up to 1 GW, Feb 2026), Fyresdal Norway (construction from Q1 2026), Vaasa Finland (coming soon). 33. **O/M.** Historical M&A is small and asset-level: Munich (from Vodafone, Jan 2019), Global Data Center Israel (2021), GTN Indonesia (2022), Chayora minority (2021). Growth since 2020 is otherwise **organic, land-led and power-led**. [S47, S48, S51, S57]
34. **O/M.** Regulatory exposure active in 2026: Texas audit "pause" on data-centre interconnection (3 Aug 2026, broadened 21 Sep 2026; EdgeConneX publicly committed to comply on 7 Aug 2026); Ohio Supreme Court order putting the Ashville agreement to a referendum (7 Aug 2026); AEP Ohio data-centre tariff (Jul 2025); Irish CRU connection policy (Dec 2025). [S41, S63, S64, S65]
35. **U.** Revenue, EBITDA, capex, net debt, leverage and sponsor mark are **not public** on any matched perimeter. No enterprise value is derived in this file.
36. **O/M.** Headquarters are stated as Northern Virginia, Amsterdam and Singapore (APAC HQ). [S17]
