## 0. Read first: three limits on this recode

**0.1 The v0.3 ontology pack was not supplied.** The only upload was the task brief (`CLAUDE_B01A_R15_INDEPENDENT_BLIND_RECODE.md`). `DC_MA_Ontology_v0.3_DeepResearch_Text_Pack.md` was not in the upload folder, the repository or anywhere on the filesystem. So I cannot quote exact frozen Concept IDs, and I have not made any up. Every `ConceptID` cell reads `UNMAPPED`. The `ConceptLabel` cell carries a plain descriptive label so a coder holding the pack can map it one-to-one. Facets use the chain slots from the brief: Condition, Motive, Mechanism, Resource, Structure/Financing, Outcome and Policy. I applied rules R06, R27, R37 and R38 as the brief describes them:
- **R06:** every Primary driver carries a counterfactual.
- **R27:** a policy counts as causal only through a stated chain from policy to mechanism to deal.
- **R37:** a non-terminal motive is traced to the terminal motive it serves.
- **R38:** integration intent needs evidence dated on or before closing.

If the pack's wording of these rules differs, the affected rows are flagged in Section 5 for re-checking.

**0.2 Primary documents were read through a search index.** The network policy blocked direct fetches from cninfo.com.cn, szse.cn, cnstock.com, 21jingji.com, chnfund.com, the Chongqing AMR site and blackstone.com. Filing content was captured through search-index extraction of those same documents. The source register names the underlying primary document wherever the index identified one. No claim is graded above SourceConfidence **M** unless two independent sources agree.

**0.3 Scales used.**
- **Likelihood** is my confidence that the node is a true causal part of the deal: H (>75%), M (40 to 75%), L (<40%).
- **SourceConfidence** is H (primary, wording captured), M (primary via extraction, or strong secondary) or L (single secondary).
- **D** marks a derived figure computed from sourced figures.
